# VIEWSHIFT original cut-ins

These five short, silent pixel animations were drawn from scratch by `scripts/render-cutins.cjs` as 320 × 180 RGB frames and encoded locally with FFmpeg. They are original game art, not Google Veo output and not footage or characters from another work.

| Name | Length | Scene |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `op` | 4 s | Cave arch, lantern lighting, original tiny staff character enters |
| `event` | 2 s | Stone tiles, a pixel exclamation mark, and moving star particles |
| `safety` | 2 s | Rising shield and an expanding safety ring; no injury imagery |
| `social` | 2 s | Two original characters and speech bubbles meet |
| `ending` | 4 s | Mossy stone treasure chest and spreading gold stars |

Each name has an H.264 MP4, VP9 WebM, and PNG poster. All clips are silent. React supplies captions separately. Rerun `node scripts/render-cutins.cjs` after changing the source renderer.
